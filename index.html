What we do

Desktop apps

Programs you install and run on your own computer. RepViewer, the recipe manager in the screenshot, is one of ours. This is the right choice when you need to work with local files or plug in a device.

  • Local files
  • Works offline
  • USB and Bluetooth devices

Web apps and websites

Things that open in a browser, with nothing to install. Our Rubik's cube solver controls a robot over USB straight from the page. We also make plain websites like this one.

  • Phone and computer
  • Nothing to install
  • Talks to hardware

Projects

Two things we have built.

RepViewer

A desktop app that keeps your recipes in one place. Save recipes from the web or write your own, sort them into folders, search them and rate them from one to five hearts. Cooking mode stops the screen from going to sleep while you cook.

  • Desktop app
  • Folders and search
  • Cooking mode

Cube solver

A web page that runs a robot solving a Rubik's cube. It shows the cube in 3D, connects to a Gan smart cube and two LEGO SPIKE hubs, and sends the moves to the motors.

  • Web app
  • Web Serial
  • LEGO SPIKE
